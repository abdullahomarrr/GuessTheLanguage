# Source notes

- **Glottolog 5.3** — language/dialect identifiers, hierarchy, ISO mappings, and
  coordinates. Source license: CC BY 4.0.
- **Natural Earth** — public-domain country geometry used by the globe.
- **Lingua Libre / Wikimedia Commons** — candidate audio. Licensing is verified
  per file; the provider name alone never determines its license.
- **Tatoeba** — sentence text, direct English translations, and human sentence
  audio. Text is generally CC BY 2.0 FR or CC0; audio licences vary by recording.
  The candidate importer retains the contributor, attribution URL, sentence and
  translation licences, and audio licence separately.
- **Commissioned/native-speaker submissions** — suitable for filling catalog
  gaps when explicit rights, attribution, and review records are retained.
- **Mozilla Common Voice** — not a re-hosting source in this starter pipeline.
  Dataset access and redistribution terms require a separate legal/terms review.

Source notes were supplied with the starter archive and last reviewed there on
2026-09-20. Re-check provider terms before production ingestion.
